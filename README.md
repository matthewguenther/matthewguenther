# Matthew G. Guenther, Ph.D.

**Drug Discovery | Molecular Biology | Genomics | AI Evaluation**

I am a molecular and cell biologist with more than 20 years of academic and biotechnology research experience spanning genomics and epigenomics, oncology, RNA biology, stem cell biology, functional genomics, and drug discovery.

My research has focused on understanding disease biology, identifying and validating therapeutic targets, developing small molecule therapeutics, developing biomarkers and patient-selection strategies, and translating biological insights into drug discovery programs.
